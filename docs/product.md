# MVP requirements

Approved reference: Final Design v1.3 (`docs/design/namazimi-kildim-v1.3.html`).

Eight approved screens: clock, tracking, daily times, day detail, settings, welcome, location, notifications.
Paper beige #F5EEE3, emerald #086347, text #40362B, muted #82735E, inactive #E5DACA, border #DED2C0, selected #E2E9DA.
Minimalist digital 24-hour circular prayer clock; active period and countdown; emerald rectangular Kıldım button; five small tracking squares; week/month/year heatmap; equal-width daily time rows; sunrise separate; numbered connected onboarding circles; flags beside languages; city selection cards.
Six languages TR EN NL FR DE AR including RTL. Manual or optional GPS location; optional notifications. No mandatory sign-in. Local records only.
Future: widgets, compass, dark mode, qaza tracking, optional sync, watch apps and Ramadan calendar.
Calendar heatmaps start on Monday with weekday labels and outline today. The 12/24-hour setting applies to every displayed time.
